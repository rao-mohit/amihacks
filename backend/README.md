# 🍲 SurplusToShelter

### Connecting Surplus Food with People in Need

SurplusToShelter is a food rescue platform designed to connect food donors with NGOs/shelters and volunteer drivers. The platform helps manage surplus food donations, recipient matching, pickup, delivery, and impact tracking.

---

## 🌟 Features

- 👤 User Registration and Login
- 🍱 Food Donation Management
- 📍 Donation Location Management
- 🤝 Food Donation Matching
- 🚚 Pickup and Delivery Management
- 📊 Impact Dashboard
- 📈 Food and Meal Statistics
- 🔄 Donation Status Tracking
- 🔔 API-based Frontend and Backend Communication
