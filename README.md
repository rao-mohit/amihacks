# 🍱 SurplusToShelter

### Smart Food Rescue & Donation Management Platform

SurplusToShelter is a web-based food rescue platform designed to connect **food donors, NGOs/shelters, and volunteer drivers**.

The platform helps reduce food wastage by allowing donors to post surplus food, automatically finding a suitable nearby NGO, and managing pickup and delivery through volunteer drivers.

# 📌 Problem Statement

A large amount of edible food is wasted every day while many people and shelters need food.

The main problems are:

- Surplus food is not easily connected with people who need it.
- Donors may not know which NGO is nearby.
- NGOs have difficulty finding available food donations.
- Volunteer drivers need a simple way to manage pickups.
- There is limited tracking of the complete donation process.
- The impact of rescued food is difficult to measure.

SurplusToShelter provides a centralized platform to manage this process.
